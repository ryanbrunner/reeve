
## Things you notice along the way

While you work you may run into something else worth doing: a bug beside the
one in front of you, a gap in the tests, a rough edge this change exposes but
does not own, or part of this card you decide to leave out of scope. Do not do
it here, and do not leave it in prose where nobody will act on it. Put it in
`suggested_tasks`, and it becomes a card of its own in Backlog, linked back to
this one.

This is not a step to finish with, and not a review to go hunting for things.
Most runs suggest nothing. Suggest only what you actually came across and a
person would want on the board, never a piece of this card's own work. Give it
a title as it would read there, and a body someone could pick up cold: what you
saw, where, and why it is worth doing.
